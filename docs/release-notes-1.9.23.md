## What's New in 1.9.23

- **Clearer Fable 5 and Claude usage-credit spending** — AIQuota now shows Claude’s combined monthly usage-credit total separately from the 5-hour and 7-day plan limits. Variable costs are highlighted without guessing how much came from Fable 5 pay-as-you-go or usage after plan limits.
- **More reliable Claude sign-in** — AIQuota now validates restored web sessions before using them. Expired sessions return to a clear Sign In state instead of repeatedly retrying stale cookies or leaving the popover stuck.
